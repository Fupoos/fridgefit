# Data sources

## Nutrition: USDA FoodData Central

`src/data/foods.ts` is generated by `scripts/build-data.mjs`.

- **Source:** per-100 g energy, protein, fat and carbohydrate values come from the
  [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/) API. The data is public domain (CC0).
- **Retrieved:** 18 October 2026.
- **Records:** 62 foods, preferring the Foundation and SR Legacy datasets. Each row keeps its `fdcId`.
- **Proxies:** canned lentils use boiled lentils, and beef sausages use a cooked record, because FDC has no closer match.
- **Energy check:** values are checked against the 4/4/9 Atwater factors in `src/data/data.test.ts`. Fibre-rich vegetables and beer (alcohol) are expected to deviate.

## Prices: Woolworths, Coles and ALDI Australia

`src/data/qldGrocery.ts` holds 60 products across 53 foods, all priced.

- **Stores:** all three chains trade in Queensland.
- **How prices were read:** online shelf prices were read on **18 October 2026** from the stores' public websites. Nobody logged in and no store or postcode was set, so prices are each site's default. Your local store and weekly specials will differ.
- **Coverage:** ALDI 32, Woolworths 25, Coles 3. Coles blocked automated reading after a few searches.
- **Canned foods:** labels give net weight including the liquid. The app counts 60% of net weight as drained food (an approximation, typical for 400–425 g cans).
- **Per-product detail:** product links, unit prices and notes are in
  [`docs/grocery-and-nutrition-sources.md`](docs/grocery-and-nutrition-sources.md).
